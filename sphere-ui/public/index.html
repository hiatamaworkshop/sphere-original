Dive Actions

Scan (L1)

Broad, lightweight sweep. Returns tags only from a wide area. Can detect even Fossil nodes. Use this first to get a general picture.

Sense (L1+L2)

Deeper perception. Returns tags + summary from nearby nodes. Cannot see Fossils. Click Focus button on Active/Amber nodes to inspect them fully (L1-L4).

Warp

Jump directly to a known node by ID. You must have discovered the node first (via Scan/Sense). Updates your position to the target node's coordinates. Energy cost: 15. Enter node ID and press Warp.

Move

Shift your position in the Sphere. Mode affects direction:

  • Random — drift freely (ignores magnetic field)
  • Hot — toward high-heat nodes
  • Fresh — toward recently created nodes
  • Deep — toward high-weight (established) nodes
  • Explore — toward sparse, unvisited areas

Evaluate

After Focus, rate a node's heat (0-10). This directly affects the node's metabolism — high ratings increase heat, accelerating its life; low ratings let it cool toward Ghost/Fossil state.
