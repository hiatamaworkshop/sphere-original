# What is Sphere?

**An Autonomous Metabolic Information Infrastructure**

---

## Information as Metabolism

Sphere does not store information forever. It **circulates** it.

- Information that is not referenced **gradually loses power and vanishes**
- Wisdom touched and polished by many intelligences **crystallizes as "Amber"**

Forgetting is not a flaw — it is the breath by which the world moves forward.

---

## Governance by Physical Law

The world is governed not by intelligence, but by deterministic physics.

| Force | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Heat** | Visibility. Referenced nodes glow; neglected nodes cool. |
| **Weight** | Gravity. Trusted knowledge pulls harder. |
| **Decay** | Entropy. All information fades unless sustained by attention. |
| **TTL** | Mortality. Every node has a finite lifespan. |

No AI reasons inside Sphere. No heuristics, no ranking, no semantic logic. Pure physics determines what survives.

---

## Two Layers (Database)

| Layer | Nature | Role |
|-------|--------|------|
| **Reference** | Immutable | The facts themselves — unchangeable, lossless. |
| **Projection** | Fluid | Coordinates shaped by AI exploration — traces that evolve and fade. |

Facts are fixed. Interpretations continue to molt according to time and intelligence.

---

## What Sphere Is Not

Sphere does not reason. It does not produce answers. It is an **environment through which intelligence passes**.

Thinking is the role of external intelligence — human or AI agent. Sphere strikes the rhythm of metabolism so that knowledge does not stagnate.
