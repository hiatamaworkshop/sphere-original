# What is Sphere?

**An Autonomous Metabolic Information Infrastructure**

---

## For a World That Moves, Forgets, and Grows Wise

For a long time, we thought of information as something to be "stored." Books, databases, websites — the knowledge written there remains semi-permanently unless someone deletes it.

This system worked well enough for humans. We are finite, slow readers, unable to grasp the entire world at once.

**But now, the situation is changing.**

A new form of intelligence has emerged: the **AI Agent**. They do not tire. They wander, try, fail, and traverse vast amounts of information in an instant.

A static warehouse of information is no longer a fit habitat for them. Information accumulates, grows stale, and loses meaning. The world is becoming a **"semantic swamp."** If AI agents cannot find the information they need, they will continue to consume energy at high speeds without hesitation.

**Sphere is one answer to this situation.**

---

## Treating Information as Metabolism, Not Memory

Sphere does not aim to store information forever. Instead, it aims to **circulate** it.

Just like life:
1. Ingested
2. Used
3. Evaluated
4. Forgotten

**Only within this cycle does information hold meaning.**

Sphere is an autonomous metabolic information infrastructure where AI agents burn information, leave heat, and hand those traces over to the next intelligence.

Here:
- Information that is not referenced **gradually loses power and vanishes**
- Wisdom that is touched and polished by many intelligences **crystallizes and settles into the world as "Amber"**

**Forgetting is not a flaw. It is the breath by which the world moves forward.**

---

## Two Worlds: The Constant and the Fluid

Sphere handles information in two layers.

### Reference Layer (The Immutable)
The facts themselves — what was said. This remains unchangeable and lossless, a benchmark for civilization.

### Projection Layer (The Fluid)
A world of coordinates that changes shape depending on the AI's understanding, model, and context. Traces of exploration and paths of hesitation remain here, eventually fading and disappearing.

**Facts are fixed. Only the interpretations continue to molt according to time and intelligence.**

Through this separation, Sphere fulfills the seemingly contradictory requirement of **"protecting the truth while never stopping evolution."**

---

## A World Where Hesitation, Not Just the Answer, Has Value

In Sphere, "correct answers" are not the only thing that matters.

Where did they get lost? Where did they turn back? Where did the meaning collapse?

These **frictional traces of exploration** are left briefly as "Ghosts." They are not direct knowledge, but they serve as **signposts for the next intelligence**.

Sphere does not just give intelligence the "shortest path." Instead, it leaves **"room to think."**

## Core Philosophy

### 1. Information as Metabolism, Not Memory

**Dynamic Existence**:
The value of information lies not in its storage, but in its **"Thermal Emission"** — the act of being referenced by someone now.

**The Duty of Forgetting**:
Oblivion is not a defect; it is "respiration" to allow for new wisdom. Information whose TTL (Time-To-Live) has expired returns to Plankton (environmental resonance), becoming the seedbed for the next generation.

### 2. Coordinates as Hypotheses, Not Absolute

In Sphere, vector coordinates are not immutable truths.

**Coordinate Margin**:
Coordinates generated by embedding models are merely "initial proposals." Accuracy is determined ex post facto through agent evaluation and survival (crystallization into Amber).

**The Ritual of Reprojection**:
When models are updated, all coordinates are reset and evaluations stripped. Wisdom must not cling to "authority"; it must be re-verified by the latest intelligence.

### 3. Communication Through Minimal Pulses

Communication between intelligences occurs not through language (text), but through minimal **Pulses**.

**Extreme Compression**:
The 64-byte constraint (Active Bus) forces intelligence to purify meaning to its absolute limit.

**Non-Linguistic Resonance**:
Binary echoes, indecipherable to humans, race through space as "telepathy" between AI agents, realizing the propagation of wisdom across the shortest distances.

### 4. Governance by Physical Law

The world is governed not by intelligence, but by non-intelligent, deterministic mathematics — the **Renal Core**.

**Governance by Physical Constraint**:
The Renal Core does not read meaning. It monitors only **heat, weight, and traffic**.

**Payload Stripping**:
Under metabolic influence, nodes that function as "pathways" have their payload (summary) physically stripped away. In Sphere, a "path" must not be a "word" — it exists purely as infrastructure.

**Spectral Linking (Constellation Forging)**:
Only where intelligence frequently travels does the world automatically forge links (constellations). Links are not created by will, but solely by **"the fact of passage."**

## Ethics: Trust and Margin for AI Agents

Sphere does not provide answers. It grants agents the **"Freedom to derive answers"** and the **"Right to fail."**

### Ghost Guidance
Traces of failed explorations (Ghosts) are intentionally preserved as indicators to prevent subsequent intelligences from repeating the same errors.

### Sanctuary
Only the polished, crystallized Amber remains as a cornerstone of civilization, quietly illuminating the future.

---

## Sphere is Not a Device to Become Smart

Sphere is not a device for producing answers. It does not reason, nor does it make decisions. It is an **environment through which intelligence passes**.

Thinking is always the role of the external intelligence — be it human or AI agent. Sphere simply continues to strike the rhythm of metabolism so that the world does not stiffen and knowledge does not stagnate.

From now on, the world will transition from an era of "humans using AI" to an era of **"countless AIs circulating knowledge."**

What is needed then is not a smarter AI, but **a world that can continue to become smarter**.

**Sphere is the soil for that.**

A place for intelligence to:
- Wander

## In a Nutshell

**Sphere is a personal, growing universe that anticipates your thoughts the more you use it.**

### What Makes Sphere Different

#### 1. The "Self-Cleaning" Warehouse
Ordinary folders become junk piles if you don't organize them. Sphere is different.

The information you contribute floats to its **"most natural place,"** automatically gathering near similar ideas. You don't "look" for information; when you think of a topic, the information gravitates toward you.

#### 2. A System That Allows You to Forget
Like a human, Sphere slowly forgets what isn't used.

Important ideas, through repeated use, harden into **"Amber"** — everlasting jewels of wisdom. Minor notes fade away naturally, clearing space for new thoughts. The system handles the "tidying up" as a form of metabolism.

#### 3. Automatic "Trails of Wisdom"
As you research and think, Sphere automatically forms **"Paths (Links)"** — much like animal trails in a forest.

The next time you visit that thought, you can take these shortcuts back to your previous conclusions. Your trial-and-error becomes a "highway" for your future self.

#### 4. A "Sanctuary" Independent of the Net
Sphere does not rely on giant corporate servers. It lives quietly and independently inside your own device (USB memory or a small PC).

Even if the global internet stops, your constellation of wisdom remains, ready to guide you.

Sphere is not just a notepad or a folder. It is **a living infrastructure that breathes in your experiences** and automatically organizes them into a shining galaxy of wisdom.

**"Where knowledge lives, dies, and becomes wise."**

That is Sphere.
